OpenMathBoard is designed for math teachers and visual learners using a mouse, touch screen, or Apple Pencil. Freehand strokes can become lines, circles, and parabolas while ordinary drawing remains fluid.

It includes selection, snapping, image import, undo and redo, clipboard and image export, English and Chinese interfaces, responsive controls, and Playwright browser tests.
